### Abstract

Hymple represents a paradigm shift in cryptocurrency exchange architecture, combining the operational efficiency of centralized exchanges (CEX) with the security and sovereignty principles of decentralized exchanges (DEX). This whitepaper outlines the technical framework, economic model, and strategic vision behind Hymple's hybrid approach to digital asset trading.
