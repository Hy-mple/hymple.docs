# Audit & Transparency Model

> *Audit and Transparency Model*

Hymple adopts a policy of technical and operational transparency as a central pillar of its credibility, ensuring that users, partners and investors can independently verify the system's operation, the protocol's health, and the integrity of operations.

### 1. Independent Audits of Smart Contracts

All smart contracts used by Hymple — including execution, validation, staking and reward distribution modules — will be subjected to independent audits conducted by specialized firms.

These audits will evaluate:

- Logical and mathematical security of the contracts.
- Possible attack or manipulation vectors.
- Compliance with industry best practices.
- Robustness of protections against exploits, frontrunning and permission failures.

### 2. On-Chain Verification and Open Source (when applicable)

Hymple seeks to ensure maximum technical auditability whenever possible:

- The contract code will be published and verified on-chain, allowing open inspection by the community.
- Addresses, versions and states of the contracts will be made available in official repositories.
- Critical changes will require formal migration and public documentation.

Transparency is treated as a security tool, not just communication.

### 3. Continuous Operational Security Auditing

In addition to contract audits, Hymple will use continuous monitoring practices:

- Critical logs published or verifiable (cryptographically anchored).
- Periodic penetration testing of backend systems and APIs.
- Reviews of server, devops and infrastructure configurations.
- Automated monitoring of integrity and anomalous behavior.

Summarized reports of these checks will be released on a regular cycle.

### 4. Economic Transparency of the HYM Token

To ensure trust in the economic model, Hymple will maintain:

- Publication of on-chain HYM metrics.
- Public monitoring of circulating supply.
- Clear disclosures about burning, buybacks, distribution and rewards.
- Periodic accountability of allocations and unlocks.

These data will be presented on the HYM Transparency Dashboard.

### 5. Commitment to Responsibility and Open Communication

Hymple maintains a commitment to operate with clear and accessible communication mechanisms:

- Detailed public documentation.
- Official announcements about critical updates, maintenance and technical changes.
- A dedicated channel for security disclosures.
- Immediate and transparent disclosure in case of detected incidents or vulnerabilities.

The goal is to ensure that any participant can independently assess the platform's level of security and compliance.
