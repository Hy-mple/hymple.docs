# System Limitations & Assumptions

> *Operational assumptions and technical limits that define the real scope of the system*

### 1. Overview

Although Hymple is designed to provide cutting-edge performance, security and scalability, it is important to acknowledge that no system is completely immune to technical, operational or market limitations. This section defines the assumptions underlying the platform's operation and describes known limitations, allowing users, investors and partners to have realistic expectations.

### 2. System Assumptions

The operation of Hymple depends on a number of technical and operational premises, including:

**Integrity of external blockchain infrastructures**

On-chain operations, settlements and smart contract verifications assume that the integrated blockchains maintain adequate security, consistency and availability.

**Network connectivity and availability**

Users are expected to have reliable internet access. Network outages may affect order execution or data updates.

**Efficient execution of smart contracts**

All contracts used will be audited, but it is assumed that their execution will follow expected standards and that unknown vulnerabilities will not be exploited.

**Rational behavior of participants**

The rewards, staking and liquidity model assumes that most users act responsibly and do not seek to manipulate incentive mechanisms.

**Internal monitoring and security systems**

The secure operation of the platform depends on servers, monitoring and internal alerts. Catastrophic hardware or software failures can impact the user experience despite redundancies implemented.

**Adoption of security practices by the user**

It is assumed that users keep their private keys secure and follow good practices to protect against phishing, malware and other attacks.

### 3. Known System Limitations

Despite the advanced hybrid design, Hymple has known technical and operational limitations:

**Partial dependence on centralized components**

The off-chain order book, APIs and matching engine servers are centralized, which may introduce risks of latency or temporary failures.

**Scalability limits in extreme scenarios**

During times of market congestion or volume spikes far exceeding forecasts, latency may increase and orders may be delayed.

**Blockchain interoperability limitations**

Not all digital assets or blockchains can be supported immediately. Adding new protocols requires additional development and auditing.

**Risk of market manipulation in low liquidity**

Assets with low liquidity may be susceptible to manipulated price movements, despite the platform's anti-manipulation protections.

**Not a 100% offline or trustless solution**

Although Hymple is non-custodial and transparent, the off-chain layer and the initial centralized governance introduce a necessary degree of trust.

**Legal and regulatory limitations**

Hymple's global operation depends on compliance with local and international regulations, which may evolve. This may impact the availability of features in certain regions.

### 4. Mitigation Strategies

To reduce the impacts of the limitations, Hymple implements:

- Redundancy and failover: multiple servers and geographic regions.
- Continuous monitoring: automatic alerts and system health dashboards.
- Periodic auditing: technical and smart contract reviews.
- Anti-manipulation mechanisms: limits, checks and real-time alerts.
- Clear governance policies: upgrade and integration decisions are documented and auditable.

### 5. Conclusion

The transparent acknowledgement of limitations and assumptions reinforces Hymple's credibility, helping users and investors understand the ecosystem's operation, inherent risks and the mitigation strategies adopted.

This commitment to transparency is fundamental to the hybrid model, balancing technological innovation, security and responsibility.
