# Burn Policy

> *Burn Policy and Scheduled Supply Reduction*

Hymple's Burn Policy was developed to create a sustainable, transparent economic model focused on long-term value for HYM token holders. The goal is to gradually reduce the circulating supply while preserving the token's real utility within the platform.

---

### 1. Strategic Objective of the Burn

The burning of HYM tokens has two central purposes:

- **Reduce supply over time**, contributing to a controlled deflationary model.
- **Align platform growth with token value increase**, reinforcing the relationship between using Hymple and appreciation of HYM.

This mechanism creates a cycle in which platform adoption directly benefits the token.

---

### 2. Source of Burned Tokens

The main source of HYM burn will be:

**Percentage of the platform's trading fees (10%)**, allocated exclusively for purchasing tokens on the market and executing scheduled burns.

This process ensures that supply reduction is directly connected to actual user activity.

---

### 3. Buyback & Burn Model

Hymple will adopt a transparent process of automatic repurchase of HYM using part of operating revenue:

- A predetermined fraction of the fees is accumulated.
- The platform uses these funds to repurchase HYM on the market.
- Acquired tokens are sent to an address without a private key, characterizing irreversible burn.
- All operations will be verifiable on-chain.

This model strengthens the token proportionally to platform demand.

---

### 4. Frequency of Burns

To ensure predictability and transparency:

**Burns will be carried out in regular cycles** (monthly or biweekly, as defined at launch).

Each burn event will be accompanied by a public report with:

- Amount burned
- Destination address
- Source of the funds used
- Transaction hashes

---

### 5. Limits, Sustainability and Flexibility

The burn policy was designed to be deflationary, yet sustainable, avoiding excessive impacts or artificial distortions in the market.

- Percentages may be adjusted in the future according to the platform's evolution.
- Hymple will not carry out arbitrary or unexpected burns — any change in the policy will be documented and formally announced.
- Technical governance and legal responsables will ensure transparency in any future alteration.

---

### 6. Commitment to On-Chain Transparency

All burn events will be publicly verifiable through:

- Official burn address
- Transaction hashes
- Aggregated reports published on HYM Transparency Dashboard

**Nothing is done off-chain** — the community can audit the entire process.
