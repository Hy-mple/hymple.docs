# Tokenomics

> *Economic architecture and sustainability of the HYM token.*

HYM is the native token that supports the entire Hymple ecosystem, acting as
a governance asset, incentive mechanism and the project's economic pillar.

The tokenomics structure was designed with a focus on:

- Long-term sustainability;
- Mass adoption with balanced incentives;
- Active community participation;
- Distribution with a deflationary tendency over time.

### 🪙 Strategic Functions of the HYM Token

- **Decentralized governance:** each HYM token grants voting power in
strategic decisions related to the protocol.
- **Rewards and incentives:** fair and predictable distribution for those who use and
strengthen the platform.
- **Staking:** participation in pools with rewards in HYM, encouraging
long-term commitment.
- **Fee reductions and premium benefits:** users who use HYM obtain
reduced fees and access to advanced features.
- **Deflationary mechanism:** part of the platform fees is used for
periodic buybacks and burns, reducing the circulating supply in a
sustainable way.

### 📈 Total Token Distribution

The total supply of 70 million HYM tokens will be distributed as follows:

| Category | Amount | Percentage | Description |
|----------|--------|------------|-------------|
| Ecosystem Rewards Program | 15.000.000 | 21,4% | Rewards for users who interact with the platform (trading, staking, liquidity, referrals). |
| Staking Rewards | 15.000.000 | 21,4% | Long-term rewards for staking participants. |
| Airdrops | 2.000.000 | 2,9% | Initial incentive for community formation. |

| Treasury & Governance Reserve | 5.000.000 | 7,1% | Institutional reserve for governance and contingencies. |
| Team Members | 7.000.000 | 10% | Long-term incentives for the technical and operational team. |
| Pre-Sale | 5.000.000 | 7,1% | Private sale . |
| Public Sale | 5.000.000 | 7,1% | Public sale at TGE. |
| Liquidity & Market Stability Fund | 8.000.000 | 11,4% | Provision of liquidity and pair stability. |

### ⏳ Release Schedule and Vesting

The Platform Usage Rewards (15M) and the Staking Rewards
(15M) will be released gradually following a fixed annual cap.

**Important:**

- These tokens will not be minted over time.
- The entire amount is created at the TGE and allocated to dedicated, locked wallets
and intended exclusively for their purposes — ensuring predictable distribution.
- The actual distribution may be lower than the annual cap, depending on platform activity
and demand for the programs.

Main guidelines:

- More intense release in the early years to encourage adoption and liquidity;
- Progressive reduction over time, following a deflationary model;
- Dynamic adjustments according to actual user engagement.

Additional schedules:

- Team: 12 months cliff + 36 months linear vesting;
- Partners: 12 months cliff + 24 months monthly linear vesting;
- Private/Public Sale: controlled unlocking to avoid selling pressure;

### 💰 Economic Sustainability

- **Supply control:** gradual and deflationary model limits inflation.
- **Buybacks and burns:** part of the fees is destined for the permanent removal of
tokens from the market.
- **Circular economy:** tokens return to the ecosystem via fees, staking and
rewards.
- **Community management:** economic parameters can be revised through on-chain governance.

### 🧭 Governance

The HYM token acts as a governance mechanism, allowing voting on decisions such as:

- Economic parameters, fees and distributions;
- Listings and new pairs;
- Use of the treasury;
- Technical evolution of the protocol.

Governance is fully transparent and progressively evolves toward
greater decentralization.

### ✅ Conclusion

HYM Tokenomics combines robust initial incentives with controlled and deflationary
distribution over time, promoting a balance between
growth, liquidity and sustainable appreciation.

The model aligns the interests of users, developers and investors, providing
support for the longevity and economic resilience of the Hymple ecosystem.
